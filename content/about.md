«_My career has taken me from system technician to programmer, and from agency life into the public sector. With a strong foundation in system engineering, software architecture, and programming, I've worked across diverse technical environments and challenges._

_Today, I'm building my path in purple security, combining offensive and defensive perspectives to better understand how systems fail - and how they can be protected. My goal is to help make technology safer and more reliable, always with the awareness that security is ultimately about protecting people, not just systems._

_I'm particularly interested in the intersection of technology, security, and human behaviour. This blog is a place where I share insights, experiences, and lessons learned from my journey, with a strong focus on collaboration, ethics, and the humans behind the code._»
